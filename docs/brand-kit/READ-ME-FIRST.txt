CARLALA BRAND KIT (v1, November 2026)
=====================================
Full rules: Carlala-brand-guide.pdf

Colours:  Coral #FF6A4D · Plum #24163A · Blush #FFF4EF · Peach #FFDED3 · Lilac #EBE3FB · Grey plum #675A74
Font:     Bricolage Grotesque (free, Google Fonts). Fallback: Arial.
Taglines: EN "The car next door, by the hour."  RO "Mașina de lângă tine, cu ora."

WHICH FILE WHERE
- Stripe (Settings > Business > Branding):
    Icon  -> icon/png/carlala-icon-square-512.png
    Logo  -> logo/png/carlala-logo-1200px.png
    Brand colour #FF6A4D, accent colour #24163A
- Brevo (sender logo / email templates):
    Logo   -> email/email-logo-600x216-white-bg.png
    Header -> email/email-header-1200x300.png (display it 600 px wide)
- Profile picture (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Google, WhatsApp Business):
    icon/png/carlala-icon-square-800.png   (safe for round crops)
- Facebook cover:   social/facebook-cover-1640x624.png
- LinkedIn banner:  social/linkedin-banner-1584x396.png
- X (Twitter):      social/x-twitter-header-1500x500.png
- Google Business:  logo icon/png/carlala-icon-square-800.png, cover social/google-business-cover-1024x576.png
- Instagram post:   social/instagram-post-1080.png
- Link preview image (websites, WhatsApp shares): social/og-image-1200x630.png
- Website favicon:  favicon/ (favicon.ico, icon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png)
- Print, stickers, signage, car wrap: the .svg files in logo/svg (vector, any size)
- Word / PowerPoint / documents: logo/png/carlala-logo-1200px.png (transparent)

FOLDERS
logo/svg   vector masters (text converted to shapes: no font needed)
logo/png   transparent PNGs at 600/1200/2400 px wide, plus versions on blush and plum
icon/      app icon: rounded (coral), square (coral, round-crop safe), plum
social/    ready-made banners and covers
email/     email header and logo
favicon/   website icons
